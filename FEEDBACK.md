Suggested script for a tester (10 minutes):
1. Pick three examples from the dropdown, including one marked `hard`. Does the route make sense?
2. Type a report from your own experience. Was it routed where you would send it?
3. Raise "Cost of missing an urgent case" to $3,000. What changes?
4. Turn on the new `temperature_excursion` category and try a cold-chain report.
5. What would stop you from trusting this in production?
